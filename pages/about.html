About KPI Pro

Performance Management, Made Structured

KPI Pro is a web-based Key Performance Indicator management system built to help organizations monitor, manage, and evaluate employee performance without the chaos of manual spreadsheets.

Our Mission

In many organizations, tracking KPI progress manually is inefficient, error-prone, and difficult to manage. KPI Pro addresses this by providing a centralized digital platform where every KPI-related activity — from assignment to verification — happens in one structured workflow.


We believe performance management should be transparent for staff, effortless for managers, and trustworthy for the organization as a whole.

Project Objectives

  • Design a web-based system for managing and tracking KPI performance
  • Provide managers a platform to assign, update, and verify KPIs efficiently
  • Enable staff to monitor assigned KPIs, update progress, and submit evidence
  • Visualize KPI achievements through an interactive dashboard
  • Implement secure user authentication and profile management

What We Value

Clarity

Every goal, deadline, and metric is visible to the people who own it — no guessing, no shadow spreadsheets.

Accountability

Role-based workflows make it clear who assigns, who delivers, and who verifies each KPI.

Evidence-Based

Progress claims are backed by uploaded evidence and manager verification, not just self-reporting.
