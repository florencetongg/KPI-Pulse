Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

KPI Pro respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

During registration and platform usage, we may collect:

  • Full name

Account Information

  • Login credentials (encrypted passwords)
  • Account activity logs
  • Authentication records

KPI Information

  • Assigned KPIs
  • KPI progress data
  • KPI submissions
  • KPI reviews and approvals
  • KPI history records

Uploaded Content

  • Supporting documents
  • Evidence files
  • File metadata

3. Data Storage and Security

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Audit Logs

These logs may be retained even after certain records are archived.

6. User Rights

  • Correct account details

7. Cookies and Session Data

  • Enhance user experience

8. Data Retention

9. Third-Party Services
